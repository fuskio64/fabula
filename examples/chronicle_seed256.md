# What Happened in Cold Hollow

This is the record of Cold Hollow, which held 10 people at its opening. It covers 192 of their days. None of it was made up.

7 times, someone in Cold Hollow went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 11 favors in Cold Hollow had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

3 bonds were made in Cold Hollow in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

One child was born in Cold Hollow in this time.

By the close of this record, Cold Hollow counted more souls than it had at the opening.

## Linde and Runa: a love

Linde was tireless and talkative. Runa was idle and tight-fisted.

It began in spring. Linde shared bread with Runa in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. A year went by. By spring nothing had been resolved. What had grown between Linde and Runa became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. By then it was autumn. A child — Asha — came to them. There are things that arrive without ceremony and are felt for years. The bread came 5 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Tomes and Coll: an unfinished business

Tomes, reserved and tireless, was known to everyone. Coll was humble and open-handed.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. Tomes turned Coll away empty-handed, and the whole village felt colder for it. Not many days passed. It came to hard words between Tomes and Coll, in the open street. When winter came again, the story had not ended. Tomes shared bread with Coll in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Generosity is not always a matter of character. The door stayed shut 3 more times. The village counted each one.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Arvid and Joren: a love

Arvid was idle and proud. Joren was humble and tight-fisted.

The first anyone knew of it was in winter. When Arvid's stores ran low, Joren filled the basket without being asked. A year went by. By winter nothing had been resolved. What had grown between Arvid and Joren became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. The bread came 2 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

In the end, Linde and Runa were still finding reasons to cross each other's paths in Cold Hollow. Tomes and Coll were still finding reasons not to.
