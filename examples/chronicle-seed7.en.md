# The Chronicle of Thornby

Thornby held 10 souls, and this record covers 192 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

18 times, someone in Thornby went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

## Yse and Ulric: an estrangement

Yse, humble and tight-fisted, was known to everyone. Ulric was tight-fisted and tireless.

It began in winter. Yse went hungry to Ulric's door and came back with nothing. Not many days passed. When Yse's stores ran low, Ulric filled the basket without being asked. Spring came. Yse and Ulric quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. And so it went on, season after season, until counting lost its point.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Ulric and Mara: an unfinished business

Mara, talkative and tireless, was known to everyone.

It began in winter. Mara went hungry to Ulric's door and came back with nothing. By then it was summer. Ulric and Mara quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. Winter came. When Mara's stores ran low, Ulric filled the basket without being asked. It happened 2 more times after that, as if grudges kept a calendar of their own.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Rafe and Mara: a friendship

Rafe was idle and reserved.

It began in spring. Mara shared bread with Rafe in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. And so it went on, season after season; some things do not need remarking to continue.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

When the record ends, Rafe and Mara were as close as two people get in Thornby; Rafe and Ulric still were not speaking.
