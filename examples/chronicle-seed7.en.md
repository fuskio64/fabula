# The Chronicle of Thornby

Thornby held 10 souls, and this record covers 192 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

15 times, someone in Thornby went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 15 favors in Thornby had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

## Rafe and Liesl: a debt unpaid

Rafe, idle and reserved, was known to everyone. Liesl was tight-fisted and tireless.

It began in spring. When Rafe's stores ran low, Liesl filled the basket without being asked. Summer came. Rafe went hungry to Liesl's door and came back with nothing. Autumn came. The favor sat heavy on Rafe. It is a hard thing to owe, and Rafe took to avoiding Liesl's eye. By then it was spring. Rafe and Liesl quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. It happened 3 more times after that, as if grudges kept a calendar of their own.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Rafe and Mara: a friendship

Mara was talkative and tireless.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. When Rafe's stores ran low, Mara filled the basket without being asked. Winter came. Rafe had not forgotten the old kindness, and when want came to Mara's house, it was returned without a word about it. And so it went on, season after season; some things do not need remarking to continue.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Aldous and Liesl: a debt unpaid

Aldous was proud and idle.

It began in winter. Liesl turned Aldous away empty-handed, and the whole village felt colder for it. Soon afterwards. Liesl shared bread with Aldous in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Summer came. Aldous had never returned what Liesl once gave, and the owing curdled, the way gratitude does when it is carried too long. It happened 2 more times after that, as if grudges kept a calendar of their own.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

When the record ends, Yse and Mara were as close as two people get in Thornby; Rafe and Liesl still were not speaking.
