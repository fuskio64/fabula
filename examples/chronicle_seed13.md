# The Chronicle of Fenwick

This is the record of Fenwick, which held 10 people at its opening. It covers 192 of their days. None of it was made up.

8 times, someone in Fenwick went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 12 favors in Fenwick had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

One soul in Fenwick did not live through these years. A village carries its dead quietly, which is how the dead end up carrying the village.

Asha came to Fenwick as someone the village did not know yet. The village is always uncertain what to make of someone with no history here.

2 bonds were made in Fenwick in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

## Dara and Hadwin: a love

Dara was humble and open-handed. Hadwin was humble and idle.

It began in spring. Dara shared bread with Hadwin in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. The bread Dara once gave came back to Dara's own door, and it was Hadwin who carried it. By then it was spring. What had grown between Dara and Hadwin became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. The bread came again, without a word about it.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Cray and Tam: a friendship

Cray, idle and humble, was known to everyone. Tam, tireless and talkative, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. When Cray's stores ran low, Tam filled the basket without being asked. The bread came 6 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Donat and Rafe: a reconciliation

Donat was idle and tight-fisted. Rafe, talkative and tight-fisted, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. Rafe turned Donat away empty-handed, and the whole village felt colder for it. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. When Donat's stores ran low, Rafe filled the basket without being asked.

Whatever had been broken, they mended it better than new.

Donat did not outlive this chronicle; a sudden blow, when there was nothing left to absorb it.

In the end, Dara and Tam were still finding reasons to cross each other's paths in Fenwick. Cray and Asha were still finding reasons not to.
