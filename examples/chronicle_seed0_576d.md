# A Record of Alderford

Alderford held 10 souls at the opening of this record, and it covers 576 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

34 times, someone in Alderford went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 5 favors in Alderford had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

12 souls in Alderford did not live through these years. A village carries its dead quietly, which is how the dead end up carrying the village.

One person left Alderford during this time, before the record closed.

None of the 10 who opened this record were still in Alderford at its close. The village that remained was an entirely different one.

8 strangers came to Alderford in this time. The village is always uncertain what to make of someone with no history.

8 bonds were made in Alderford in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

2 children were born in Alderford in this time.

## The Village

The winter was a hungry one. More than half the houses in Alderford had lean pantries, and no one pretended otherwise.

That was not the only lean season. Alderford counted 11 in the course of these years — hunger was a recurring visitor, not a single hard winter.

Alderford came through that summer with full stores. Those who remember the lean years never quite take it for granted.

## Miren: a stranger

Miren was tireless and humble.

Miren arrived in autumn. Alderford had not been expecting anyone. By then it was winter. When Miren's stores ran low, Liesl filled the basket without being asked. Not many days passed. The bread Liesl once gave came back to Liesl's own door, and it was Miren who carried it. By then it was spring. Miren shared bread with Pell in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten.

Miren had arrived knowing no one and left a member of Alderford, in whatever way that word applies.
