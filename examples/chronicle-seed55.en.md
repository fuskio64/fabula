# The Chronicle of Ashgate

Ashgate held 10 souls, and this record covers 192 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

9 times, someone in Ashgate went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 14 favors in Ashgate had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

## Wren and Ulric: a debt unpaid

Wren was reserved and humble. Ulric was tight-fisted and idle.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. Wren shared bread with Ulric in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Soon afterwards. Ulric owed Wren the kind of debt nobody writes down, and paid it with a closed door. Nobody said anything. Everybody counted it. Spring came. Ulric had never returned what Wren once gave, and the owing curdled, the way gratitude does when it is carried too long. Winter came. It came to hard words between Wren and Ulric, in the open street.

No one mended it. By the end they passed each other without a word.

## Tomes and Petra: a friendship

Tomes, humble and idle, was known to everyone. Petra, open-handed and talkative, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. When Tomes's stores ran low, Petra filled the basket without being asked. Autumn came. The bread Petra once gave came back to Petra's own door, and it was Tomes who carried it. And so it went on, season after season; some things do not need remarking to continue.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Yse and Ulric: a friendship

Yse, open-handed and talkative, was known to everyone.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. When Ulric's stores ran low, Yse filled the basket without being asked. And it happened 5 more times, without anyone making a fuss of it; kindness keeps a calendar too.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

When the record ends, Liesl and Petra were as close as two people get in Ashgate; Wren and Ulric still were not speaking.
