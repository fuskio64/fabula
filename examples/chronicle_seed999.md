# What Happened in Millhollow

Millhollow held 10 souls at the opening of this record, and it covers 192 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

16 times, someone in Millhollow went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 14 favors in Millhollow had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

3 bonds were made in Millhollow in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

## Ilde and Vesna: a debt unpaid

Ilde was tireless and tight-fisted. Vesna was talkative and proud.

The first anyone knew of it was in autumn. Vesna went hungry to Ilde's door and came back with nothing. Winter came. Ilde shared bread with Vesna in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. By then it was spring. The favor sat heavy on Vesna. It is a hard thing to owe, and Vesna took to avoiding Ilde's eye. Summer came. It came to hard words between Ilde and Vesna, in the open street. They fought 6 more times. It never seemed to settle anything.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Yse and Linde: a debt unpaid

Yse, proud and reserved, was known to everyone. Linde, open-handed and tireless, was known to everyone.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. When Yse's stores ran low, Linde filled the basket without being asked. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. The favor sat heavy on Yse. It is a hard thing to owe, and Yse took to avoiding Linde's eye. Spring came. It came to hard words between Yse and Linde, in the open street. They fought 4 more times. It never seemed to settle anything.

No one mended it. By the end they passed each other without a word.

## Leif and Donat: a love

Leif, idle and reserved, was known to everyone. Donat, humble and open-handed, was known to everyone.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. When Leif's stores ran low, Donat filled the basket without being asked. When winter came again, the story had not ended. Leif had not forgotten the old kindness, and when want came to Donat's house, it was returned without a word about it. Soon afterwards. What had grown between Leif and Donat became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. The bread came 5 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

When the record ends, Leif and Donat were as close as two people get in Millhollow; Ilde and Linde still were not speaking.
