# What Happened in Millhollow

Millhollow held 10 souls at the opening of this record, and it covers 192 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

16 times, someone in Millhollow went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 14 favors in Millhollow had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

3 bonds were made in Millhollow in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

## The Village

The winter was a hungry one. More than half the houses in Millhollow had lean pantries, and no one pretended otherwise.

That summer, the village was well-fed and it showed — in longer evenings, in more visiting, in quarrels that did not quite start.

## Ilde and Vesna: a debt unpaid

Ilde was tireless and tight-fisted. Vesna, talkative and proud, was known to everyone.

It began in autumn. Ilde turned Vesna away empty-handed, and the whole village felt colder for it. By then it was winter. Ilde shared bread with Vesna in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Even tight fists open, sometimes. By then it was spring. Vesna had never returned what Ilde once gave, and the owing curdled, the way gratitude does when it is carried too long. By then it was summer. Ilde and Vesna quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. They fought 6 more times. It never seemed to settle anything.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Yse and Linde: a debt unpaid

Yse was proud and reserved. Linde was open-handed and tireless.

It began in spring. Linde shared bread with Yse in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Another winter settled in. The cold version of everything returned. Yse had never returned what Linde once gave, and the owing curdled, the way gratitude does when it is carried too long. By then it was spring. Yse and Linde quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. They fought 4 more times. It never seemed to settle anything.

No one mended it. By the end they passed each other without a word.

## Leif and Donat: a love

Leif, idle and reserved, was known to everyone. Donat was humble and open-handed.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. Donat shared bread with Leif in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Winter came back, and with it the old matter came back too. The bread Donat once gave came back to Donat's own door, and it was Leif who carried it. Soon afterwards. What had grown between Leif and Donat became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. The bread came 5 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

In the end, Leif and Donat were still finding reasons to cross each other's paths in Millhollow. Ilde and Linde were still finding reasons not to.
