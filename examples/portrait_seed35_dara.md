# Dara

*Founder of Copse End. Tight-fisted and reserved. Left the village.*

## Character

- Tireless in the fields — the kind of person who works without being watched.
- Reluctant to give; not cruel, but counted their stores.
- Proud — the kind of person who would rather go hungry than ask.
- Reserved; kept what they knew to themselves.

## What Happened

- *Winter, year 1:* quarreled with Sorel, Ilde, Casper, and 3 others.
- *Spring, year 2:* quarreled with Eskel.

## How the Village Saw Them

At the time Dara left, they were poorly regarded by most of the village.
Most estranged from Wren.

## What the Village Lost

The fields Dara tended were the best-kept in Copse End. That kind of knowledge does not write itself down.
