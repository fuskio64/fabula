# The Chronicle of Cold Hollow

Cold Hollow held 10 souls at the opening of this record, and it covers 384 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

22 times, someone in Cold Hollow went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 15 favors in Cold Hollow had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

5 bonds were made in Cold Hollow in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

2 children were born in Cold Hollow in this time.

## Tam and Orin: a debt unpaid

Tam, talkative and tireless, was known to everyone. Orin was proud and idle.

It began in autumn. Tam shared bread with Orin in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Winter came. Orin owed Tam the kind of debt nobody writes down, and paid it with a closed door. Nobody said anything. Everybody counted it. Not many days passed. The favor sat heavy on Orin. It is a hard thing to owe, and Orin took to avoiding Tam's eye. By then it was spring. Word reached Tam that Orin had been speaking of the matter behind closed doors. It was Orin who carried the tale. Summer came. It came to hard words between Tam and Orin, in the open street. Soon afterwards. Hadwin sat them both at one table and would not let either leave angry. Something loosened. Summer came. It took Bryn, whom they both trusted, to make them speak again. Winter came. It took Bryn, whom they both trusted, to make them speak again. And so it went on, season after season, until counting lost its point.

The peace that was made did not keep; some cracks want to stay open.

## Orin and Wren: a debt unpaid

Wren, proud and talkative, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. Wren shared bread with Orin in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Spring came. The favor sat heavy on Orin. It is a hard thing to owe, and Orin took to avoiding Wren's eye. Not many days passed. Someone let Wren know what Orin had been saying. It was Orin who carried the tale. Autumn came. Orin and Wren quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. By then it was winter. It took Bryn, whom they both trusted, to make them speak again. And so it went on, season after season, until counting lost its point.

The peace that was made did not keep; some cracks want to stay open.

## Leif and Wren: a debt unpaid

Leif, idle and tight-fisted, was known to everyone.

It began in winter. When Leif's stores ran low, Wren filled the basket without being asked. Spring came. The favor sat heavy on Leif. It is a hard thing to owe, and Leif took to avoiding Wren's eye. By then it was summer. It came to hard words between Leif and Wren, in the open street. And so it went on, season after season, until counting lost its point.

No one mended it. By the end they passed each other without a word.

## Leif and Joren: a love

Joren, talkative and tight-fisted, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. When Leif's stores ran low, Joren filled the basket without being asked. By then it was summer. Leif had not forgotten the old kindness, and when want came to Joren's house, it was returned without a word about it. Autumn came. Leif went hungry to Joren's door and came back with nothing. Spring came. Leif and Joren chose each other before the season turned. The village knew before either of them said a word about it. And it happened 6 more times, without anyone making a fuss of it; kindness keeps a calendar too.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

When the record ends, Tam and Hadwin were as close as two people get in Cold Hollow; Orin and Wren still were not speaking.
