# The Chronicle of Cold Hollow

Cold Hollow held 10 souls at the opening of this record, and it covers 192 days of them. Nothing in it was invented; it was only watched, and told.

14 times, someone in Cold Hollow went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 11 favors in Cold Hollow had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

## Casper and Nissa: a friendship

Casper, open-handed and humble, was known to everyone. Nissa was open-handed and tireless.

It began in summer. Nissa shared bread with Casper in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Winter came. The bread Nissa once gave came back to Nissa's own door, and it was Casper who carried it. And so it went on, season after season; some things do not need remarking to continue.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Ilde and Casper: a friendship

Ilde was tight-fisted and tireless.

It began in spring. Ilde shared bread with Casper in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Winter came. Casper went hungry to Ilde's door and came back with nothing. And it happened 5 more times, without anyone making a fuss of it; kindness keeps a calendar too.

Whatever had been broken, they mended it better than new.

## Bertram and Liesl: a friendship

Bertram, open-handed and proud, was known to everyone. Liesl, idle and reserved, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. When Liesl's stores ran low, Bertram filled the basket without being asked. And so it went on, season after season; some things do not need remarking to continue.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

When the record ends, Bertram and Liesl were as close as two people get in Cold Hollow; Ilde and Joren still were not speaking.
