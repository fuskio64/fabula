# A Record of Graywick

This is the record of Graywick, which held 10 people at its opening. It covers 192 of their days. None of it was made up.

14 times, someone in Graywick went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 9 favors in Graywick had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

3 bonds were made in Graywick in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

One child was born in Graywick in this time.

By the close of this record, Graywick counted more souls than it had at the opening.

## Casper and Joren: an unfinished business

Casper, open-handed and reserved, was known to everyone. Joren, idle and reserved, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. When Joren's stores ran low, Casper filled the basket without being asked. When winter came again, the story had not ended. The favor sat heavy on Joren. It is a hard thing to owe, and Joren took to avoiding Casper's eye. Some seasons passed. By the time summer came, the matter had not been resolved. It came to hard words between Casper and Joren, in the open street. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. Joren owed Casper the kind of debt nobody writes down, and paid it with a closed door. Nobody said anything. Everybody counted it. Not many days passed. Eskel sat them both at one table and would not let either leave angry. Something loosened. They fought 2 more times. It never seemed to settle anything.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

## Bren and Miren: a love

Bren, idle and tight-fisted, was known to everyone. Miren, humble and reserved, was known to everyone.

It began in spring. When Bren's stores ran low, Miren filled the basket without being asked. By then it was summer. Bren went hungry to Miren's door and came back with nothing. By then it was autumn. Bren had not forgotten the old kindness, and when want came to Miren's house, it was returned without a word about it. Soon afterwards. What had grown between Bren and Miren became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. The bread came 4 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Rafe and Garth: a feud

Rafe was reserved and tight-fisted. Garth was idle and open-handed.

The first anyone knew of it was in spring. Rafe turned Garth away empty-handed, and the whole village felt colder for it. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. When Garth's stores ran low, Rafe filled the basket without being asked. By then it was spring. It came to hard words between Rafe and Garth, in the open street. By then it was summer. The bread Rafe once gave came back to Rafe's own door, and it was Garth who carried it. They fought 5 more times. It never seemed to settle anything.

An uneasy peace held between them, thin as spring ice.

When the record ends, Casper and Eskel were as close as two people get in Graywick; Casper and Rafe still were not speaking.
