# What Happened in Thornby

This is the record of Thornby, which held 10 people at its opening. It covers 192 of their days. None of it was made up.

9 times, someone in Thornby went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is its own kind of famine.

When the record closes, 11 favors in Thornby had still not been returned. The village kept those accounts in no book, which is why none of them were ever lost.

2 bonds were made in Thornby in this time. Most lasting things begin without anyone marking the moment.

## Wulf and Sable: a love

Wulf was idle and reserved. Sable was talkative and tireless.

It began in spring. When Wulf's stores ran low, Sable filled the basket without being asked. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. Wulf had not forgotten the old kindness, and when want came to Sable's house, it was returned without a word about it. Some seasons passed. By the time summer came, the matter had not been resolved. What had grown between Wulf and Sable became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that. The bread came 3 more times, without ceremony; that is what makes it bread.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.

## Runa and Tilda: a debt unpaid

Runa was idle and reserved. Tilda was open-handed and talkative.

The first anyone knew of it was in summer. When Runa's stores ran low, Tilda filled the basket without being asked. Some seasons passed. By the time winter came, the matter had not been resolved. The favor sat heavy on Runa. It is a hard thing to owe, and Runa took to avoiding Tilda's eye. Some seasons passed. By the time autumn came, the matter had not been resolved. Runa and Tilda quarreled where everyone could hear, and the village pretended not to listen. They fought again, as if the first time had been practice.

No one mended it. By the end they passed each other without a word.

## Orin and Tilda: a love

Orin, reserved and humble, was known to everyone.

The first anyone knew of it was in winter. Tilda shared bread with Orin in a lean stretch, and it was not forgotten. Not many days passed. The bread Tilda once gave came back to Tilda's own door, and it was Orin who carried it. By then it was spring. What had grown between Orin and Tilda became something declared. They shared bread and hearth after that.

They were lucky in each other, and they knew it.
