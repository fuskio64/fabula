# The Character of These Villages

*20 runs · 288 days each · 10 souls each · seeds 0–19*

---

The bread moved. On average, 46 gifts were given per run — neighbors sharing surplus without being asked, or hungry ones knocking on doors. This world is not generous by nature; it is just small enough that hunger is visible.

**What always happened:**

- A bond formed — 20 of 20 runs (100%).
- A village-wide hungry season — 20 of 20 runs (100%).
- A season of abundance most of the village shared — 20 of 20 runs (100%).
- Someone went hungry rather than ask for help — 20 of 20 runs (100%).
- Open favors still unpaid at the close — 20 of 20 runs (100%).

**What usually happened:**

- A child born — 14 of 20 runs (70%).
- A quarrel — 17 of 20 runs (85%).
- Resentment turned a debt sour — 18 of 20 runs (90%).

**What sometimes happened:**

- At least one death — 10 of 20 runs (50%).
- A quarrel resolved by a third party — 5 of 20 runs (25%).
- An act of ingratitude — 5 of 20 runs (25%).
- A stranger arrived — 9 of 20 runs (45%).

**What occasionally happened:**

- Someone driven out by collective rejection — 1 of 20 runs (5%).

---

**On average across these runs:**

45.9 gifts given  ·  34.0 quarrels  ·  3.3 bonds formed  ·  0.8 deaths  ·  3.5 lean seasons

**Population at close:**

Grew in 12 of 20 runs, Stayed the same in 7 of 20 runs, Shrank in 1 of 20 runs.

---

Most quarrels here went unmediated. Villages find it easier to take sides than to sit people down.

Only 15% of gifts were ever returned. The ledger of unspoken obligations grew faster than it shrank.

In most runs, someone went hungry rather than ask for help. Pride is not an aberration here; it is a feature of the world.

---

The structure persists. Different seeds, different names, the same shape of things. What varies is the particular — who refused whom, which winter was hard, whose pride cost them most. What holds is the world.

*(parameters: --days 288 --people 10 · variation comes from the seed alone)*
